# Credit Scoring Method

## 📌 About the Project

The **Credit Scoring Method** is a financial assessment approach used by banks and financial institutions to evaluate the creditworthiness of individuals. It helps determine how likely a person is to repay borrowed money on time.

The system analyzes various financial factors, including payment history, outstanding debts, credit utilization, length of credit history, and new credit applications, to estimate a borrower's credit risk.

The main objective of this project is to understand how credit scoring works and how financial data can be used to support faster, more consistent, and informed lending decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Evaluate the creditworthiness of borrowers.
- Understand the important factors affecting credit scores.
- Estimate the risk associated with lending money.
- Support informed loan approval decisions.
- Improve the efficiency of credit risk assessment.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Data Collection:** Collect relevant borrower information and credit history.
2. **Data Processing:** Clean and organize the collected financial data.
3. **Feature Analysis:** Examine payment history, credit utilization, debt levels, and other relevant factors.
4. **Score Calculation:** Apply a credit scoring formula or predictive model to estimate creditworthiness.
5. **Risk Assessment:** Categorize the estimated credit risk based on predefined criteria.
6. **Decision Support:** Present the results to help lenders evaluate loan applications.

## 📊 Factors Affecting Credit Scores

- **Payment History:** Records of timely or missed payments.
- **Credit Utilization:** The proportion of available credit currently being used.
- **Credit History Length:** The duration of the borrower's credit history.
- **Credit Mix:** The variety of credit accounts.
- **New Credit Applications:** Recent applications for credit.

## 🌟 Key Features

- Creditworthiness assessment.
- Analysis of financial and credit-related information.
- Credit risk estimation.
- Borrower risk categorization.
- Support for data-driven lending decisions.

## ✅ Advantages

- Helps lenders evaluate credit risk.
- Reduces the time required for credit assessment.
- Supports consistent evaluation of borrowers.
- Helps identify potential repayment risks.
